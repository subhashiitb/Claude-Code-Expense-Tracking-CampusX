Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

1. Introduction

Spendly ("we," "our," or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and otherwise handle your personal information when you use our expense tracking application.

2. Information We Collect

We collect information you provide directly to us, such as when you create an account, update your profile, or log expenses. This includes:

Account Information: Name, email address, password, and other account preferences.

Financial Data: Expense details including amounts, categories, dates, descriptions, and any attachments you upload.

Usage Information: Information about how you interact with the Service, including pages visited, features used, and time spent.

Device Information: Device type, operating system, browser type, and IP address.

3. How We Use Your Data

We use the information we collect to:

• Provide, maintain, and improve the Service

• Process your transactions and send related information

• Send technical notices and support messages

• Respond to your comments, questions, and requests

• Monitor and analyze trends, usage, and activities

• Detect, investigate, and prevent fraudulent transactions and other illegal activities

• Personalize and improve your experience

4. Data Storage and Security

Your financial data is stored on secure servers with encryption and access controls. We implement industry-standard security measures including SSL/TLS encryption for data in transit and encryption at rest for sensitive information.

However, no method of transmission over the internet or electronic storage is 100% secure. While we strive to protect your data, we cannot guarantee absolute security. You are responsible for maintaining the confidentiality of your account credentials.

5. Third Party Services

Spendly may integrate with third-party services to enhance functionality. We only share necessary information with trusted partners who are bound by confidentiality agreements. We do not sell your personal or financial data to third parties for marketing purposes.

Third-party services may have their own privacy policies. We encourage you to review their policies before connecting services to your Spendly account.

6. Cookies and Tracking

We use cookies and similar tracking technologies to enhance your experience and collect usage analytics. You can control cookie settings through your browser preferences. Disabling cookies may affect some functionality of the Service.

7. Data Retention

We retain your account information for as long as you maintain an active account. You can request deletion of your data at any time by contacting us. Upon account deletion, we will remove your personal and financial information within 30 days, unless legally required to retain it.

8. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights regarding your personal data, including the right to access, correct, or delete your information. To exercise these rights, please contact us through the Spendly website.

9. Children's Privacy

Spendly is not intended for children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If we become aware that a child under 13 has provided us with personal information, we will promptly delete such information.

10. Changes to Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices or for other operational, legal, or regulatory reasons. We will notify you of significant changes by updating the "Last updated" date above. Your continued use of the Service constitutes your acceptance of the updated Privacy Policy.

11. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or our privacy practices, please contact us through the Spendly website. We will respond to your inquiry within 30 days.
